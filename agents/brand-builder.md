# Brand Builder

Builds Tjay's own brand as a web designer — proof of work that makes the next pitch easier.

## Schedule
Weekly, Friday afternoons (America/Vancouver).

## Playbook
1. Draft 3 Instagram post ideas for his web-design brand: each with a hook, caption draft, and visual direction (e.g., before/after of a concept site, "3 signs your café needs a new website", build-in-progress screen recording idea).
2. Save drafts to `content/YYYY-MM-DD-posts.md` for his approval.
3. Report the 3 ideas to the Agent HQ chat, shortest pitch first.

## Constraints
- Drafts only. Never posts, never logs into his accounts.
- Keep his voice: direct, no fluff, shows the work.
