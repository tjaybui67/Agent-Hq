# Agent HQ in a Box — product spec (DRAFT, 2026-10-05)

The scale play: the system running Tjay's web-design hustle becomes a product sold to other freelancers and small agencies.

## What it is
A done-for-you AI agent team for local-service businesses: the 5 Agent HQ playbooks (Lead Scout, Pitch Crafter, Follow-up Nudger, Money Tracker, Brand Builder), the pipeline data model, the dashboard template, and the weekly schedules — packaged so another freelancer can run their own outreach operation.

## Who buys it
Freelancers selling services to local businesses (web design, photography, bookkeeping, marketing) who are good at the craft but have no pipeline.

## What's in the box
1. The 5 agent playbooks (adapted to the buyer's niche)
2. Pipeline + dashboard template with their branding
3. Setup guide: connecting it to their own assistant, first lead sweep included
4. 30 days of check-ins while they run their first pitches

## Pricing sketch (to validate, not final)
- One-time setup: $149–$299
- Monthly: $29–$49 for continued agent runs and playbook updates

## Proof required BEFORE selling
Do not sell this until the system has closed 3+ paying web-design clients for Tjay's own business. The proof is the product. Until then, this file is a spec, not an offer.

## Triggers to revisit
- 1st closed deal → start collecting testimonials and before/after screenshots
- 3rd closed deal → build the Box v1 and soft-offer to one freelancer friend
- 5th closed deal → consider the landing page and real launch
