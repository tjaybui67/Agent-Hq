# Battle Cards — objection handling for in-person pitches

Read this on your phone before you walk in. Short answers, honest framing, no scripts to memorize word-for-word — know the idea, say it your way.

## 1. "How old are you?"
"17. Which is exactly why I'm cheap and exactly why I'm good at this — I grew up on the internet. An agency charges two grand for a template. I built *this* [show demo] for your shop specifically."

## 2. "We already have Facebook / Instagram."
"That's the problem, actually. When someone Googles you right now, they land on a Facebook page. A one-page site turns that search into a call — services, hours, directions, tap-to-call, all in one place."

## 3. "We don't need a website."
"Your reviews say otherwise — [X] stars from [Y] reviews means people are already looking you up. Right now what they find is [Facebook page / nothing]. You're leaving walk-ins on the table."

## 4. "Too expensive."
"Fair. The starter is $45 — that's the essentials: services, hours, map, tap-to-call. Most shops go for the $95 because it adds your reviews and a gallery, but we can start small."

## 5. "Can I think about it?"
"Of course — I'll leave this flyer. Quick question so I don't guess: what would you need to see to say yes?" (Then be quiet. Let them answer.)

## 6. "Who else have you done this for?"
"You're one of my first — that's why you're getting this price instead of agency prices. And it's why I built the concept for *your* shop before asking for anything." (Honesty beats a fake portfolio every time.)

## 7. "How long does it take?"
"About a week. And nothing goes live without your approval — you see everything first."

## 8. "What about the domain and hosting?"
"Separate from the build price, and you'd approve it before I buy anything. Usually around $15–20 a year for the domain. I'll show you the exact cost first."

## Rules for all of them
- Answer short. Then stop talking.
- Never invent a number. If you don't know, say "I'll find out and text you."
- The demo on your phone does half the selling — get to it fast.
- Ten minutes in and out. Respect their day.
