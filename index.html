Battle cards

Read on your phone before you walk in. Short answers, honest framing.

"How old are you?"

"17. Which is exactly why I'm cheap and exactly why I'm good at this — I grew up on the internet. An agency charges two grand for a template. I built this [show demo] for your shop specifically."

"We already have Facebook / Instagram."

"That's the problem, actually. When someone Googles you right now, they land on a Facebook page. A one-page site turns that search into a call — services, hours, directions, tap-to-call, all in one place."

"We don't need a website."

"Your reviews say otherwise — strong reviews means people are already looking you up. Right now what they find is a Facebook page or nothing. You're leaving walk-ins on the table."

"Too expensive."

"Fair. The starter is $45 — that's the essentials: services, hours, map, tap-to-call. Most shops go for the $95 because it adds your reviews and a gallery, but we can start small."

"Can I think about it?"

"Of course — I'll leave this flyer. Quick question so I don't guess: what would you need to see to say yes?" (Then be quiet. Let them answer.)

"Who else have you done this for?"

"You're one of my first — that's why you're getting this price instead of agency prices. And it's why I built the concept for your shop before asking for anything."

"How long does it take?"

"About a week. And nothing goes live without your approval — you see everything first."

"What about the domain and hosting?"

"Separate from the build price, and you'd approve it before I buy anything. Usually around $15–20 a year for the domain. I'll show you the exact cost first."
